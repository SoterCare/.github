## 💙 Who We Are

SoterCare is a student-led developer community building open-source healthcare technology through Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, the Internet of Things, and modern software engineering.

More than the things we build, we're here to help students grow — collaborating on real projects, contributing to open source, running technical events, and mentoring the next generation of developers.

We believe the best way to learn is by building together.

## 🛠️ What We're Building

Our projects span the full stack of modern healthcare technology:

- **Healthcare Technology** — patient-centered tools that make care more accessible
- **AI & Machine Learning** — models for prediction, monitoring, and decision support
- **IoT & Edge Devices** — sensor firmware, edge gateways, and connected health devices
- **Cloud & Backend** — the platforms and APIs that power our systems
- **Web & Mobile** — cross-platform apps that put it all in people's hands

Explore our public repositories to see what we're working on right now.

## 🌱 Get Involved

Whether it's your first pull request or you maintain production systems, there's a place for you here.

1. Browse our repositories and find a project that interests you
2. Read our [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/SoterCare/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/SoterCare/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
3. Pick an issue, open a pull request, or just say hello

Contributions of every kind are welcome — code, documentation, design, research, testing, and ideas. First-time contributors are especially encouraged.
